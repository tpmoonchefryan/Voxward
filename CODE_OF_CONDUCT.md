# Code of Conduct

**English** · [简体中文](./docs/i18n/zh-CN/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · [日本語](./docs/i18n/ja/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · [한국어](./docs/i18n/ko/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · [Français](./docs/i18n/fr/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Our Pledge

We pledge to keep participation in VoxWard respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free for contributors and users across all regions, languages, backgrounds, identities, and experience levels.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate constructively across our five supported project languages (`en`, `zh-CN`, `ja`, `ko`, `fr`).
- Assume good intent while keeping technical claims backed by reproducible evidence.
- Protect privacy: never post viewer handles, private messages, credentials, or personal contact details.
- Focus feedback on code, contracts, UX, and safety rather than personal attacks.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, intimidation, doxxing, discriminatory remarks, sexually explicit comments, and deliberate disruption of discussions or review processes are not acceptable.

## Enforcement & Reporting

Report code-of-conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer (`tpmoonchefryan`) without posting accusations or private data in public Issues or Pull Requests.

The maintainer may edit or remove offending content, close discussions, or restrict repository access as appropriate, handling reports with discretion subject to safety and legal needs.

> Security vulnerabilities must be reported via [GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting](./SECURITY.md) rather than conduct or public issue channels.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1 principles without publishing personal contact addresses.
